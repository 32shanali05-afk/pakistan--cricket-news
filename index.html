📅 9 OCTOBER 2026

PAKISTAN VS SRI LANKA T20I SERIES STARTS TOMORROW!

The much-awaited T20I SERIES between PAKISTAN and SRI LANKA kicks off tomorrow, 9 OCTOBER. All three matches of the series will be played at the RAWALPINDI CRICKET STADIUM on 9, 11, AND 13 OCTOBER.

📅 8 OCTOBER 2026

SAHIBZADA FARHAN TAKES CHARGE AS NEW T20 CAPTAIN

SAHIBZADA FARHAN is all set to lead PAKISTAN in his first official series as the full-time T20I CAPTAIN. Meanwhile, senior stars like BABAR AZAM and SHAHEEN SHAH AFRIDI are not part of this T20 squad as Pakistan tests a new setup. Farhan also recently expressed high praise for former captain SARFARAZ AHMED, stating he deeply admires his leadership style.

📅 PSL 12 ANNOUNCEMENT

PSL SEASON 12 SCHEDULE & DETAILS FINALIZED

The PCB has officially announced the dates for PAKISTAN SUPER LEAGUE 12. The tournament will run from 18 MARCH TO 2 MAY 2027, featuring an expanded format with 8 TEAMS competing for the trophy.

📅 UPCOMING ODI ASSIGNMENT

PAKISTAN'S UPCOMING ODI TRI-SERIES

Following the T20 series, PAKISTAN will shift focus to the ODI FORMAT, hosting a grand TRI-SERIES featuring ENGLAND AND SRI LANKA, where SHAHEEN SHAH AFRIDI will lead the side as captain.
