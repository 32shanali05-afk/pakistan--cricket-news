🔥 6 OCTOBER 2026 — LATEST CRICKET UPDATES

📅 6 OCTOBER 2026

MAJORS UPDATE: NEW T20I CAPTAIN APPOINTED

SAHIBZADA FARHAN has been officially appointed as Pakistan's new T20I CAPTAIN. Meanwhile, SALMAN ALI AGHA has been dropped from the T20I squad. Farhan will lead the team in the upcoming 3-match series against SRI LANKA.

📅 OCTOBER 2026

PAKISTAN VS SRI LANKA T20 SERIES SCHEDULE

All three T20I MATCHES will take place in RAWALPINDI on 9 OCTOBER, 11 OCTOBER, AND 13 OCTOBER. This marks SAHIBZADA FARHAN'S debut series as full-time T20I captain.

📅 6 OCTOBER 2026

SHAHEEN RETAINED AS ODI CAPTAIN & BABAR INCLUDED

SHAHEEN SHAH AFRIDI will continue to lead the side as ODI CAPTAIN for the upcoming ODI Tri-Series featuring SRI LANKA AND ENGLAND, with star batsman BABAR AZAM also included in the squad.

📅 6 OCTOBER 2026

PAKISTAN WITHDRAWN FROM WCL 2026

Pakistani players will not participate in the WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP OF LEGENDS (WCL) 2026 because the PCB did not issue the required NOCs for the tournament.

📅 6 OCTOBER 2026

PCB CENTRAL CONTRACTS PENDING

The previous CENTRAL CONTRACTS of Pakistani players officially expired on 31 JULY, and as of 6 OCTOBER, the PCB has not yet released the list of new contracts.

📅 6 OCTOBER 2026

NIAZ STADIUM HYDERABAD TO BE HANDED OVER TO PCB

Under an agreement between the HYDERABAD MUNICIPAL CORPORATION and the PCB, NIAZ STADIUM is planned to be handed over to the board for approximately 20 YEARS to upgrade it into an international-standard venue.
