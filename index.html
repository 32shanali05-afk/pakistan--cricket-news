📅 1 OCTOBER 2026

PAKISTAN VS BANGLADESH — ASIAN GAMES SEMI-FINAL

The SEMI-FINAL showdown is locked! PAKISTAN will face BANGLADESH on 1 OCTOBER AT 5:00 AM (PAKISTAN TIME) in NISSAN, JAPAN.

Earlier, PAKISTAN reached the semi-finals based on a better ICC RANKING after their quarter-final against HONG KONG was washed out due to rain.

📅 OCTOBER 2026 SCHEDULE

PAKISTAN'S UPCOMING HOME INTERNATIONAL SERIES

The PCB has finalized the schedule for upcoming home matches. PAKISTAN will play 3 T20Is against SRI LANKA on 9, 11, AND 13 OCTOBER, followed by an ODI TRI-SERIES featuring PAKISTAN, ENGLAND, AND SRI LANKA.

📅 DOMESTIC CRICKET UPDATE

BABAR AZAM & NATIONAL STARS IN DOMESTIC CRICKET

BABAR AZAM has officially joined the OGDCL TEAM, while other national stars including NASEEM SHAH, ABDULLAH SHAFIQ, AND SAUD SHAKEEL are actively representing various departmental teams in the PRESIDENT'S TROPHY GRADE-I.

📅 2 OCTOBER 2026

PAKISTAN WOMEN'S U19 TRI-NATION SERIES

The T20 TRI-NATION SERIES involving PAKISTAN, BANGLADESH, AND SRI LANKA women's U19 teams is all set to kick off from 2 OCTOBER in FAISALABAD.
